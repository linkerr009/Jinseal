Product Overview

Built To Maintain A Reliable Seal Under Changing Conditions.

Spiral wound gaskets combine alternating layers of formed metal strip and resilient filler. The construction responds to flange movement while retaining sealing stress during temperature and pressure changes.

01Metal windingProvides structural strength and recovery.
02Soft fillerCreates a conformable sealing surface.
03Guide ringsSupport alignment and compression control.

Choose The Configuration That Fits The Flange.

What information is needed for a quotation?add

Please provide the gasket size or flange standard, metal and filler preference, working medium, temperature, pressure and order quantity. Drawings or sample photos are also helpful.

Can JinSeal manufacture from a drawing or sample?add

Yes. Custom dimensions, materials, ring structures, markings and packaging can be produced after technical confirmation.

Which filler material should I choose?add

Graphite is common for broad industrial service, while PTFE is often selected for chemical resistance. The final choice depends on medium, temperature and pressure.

Do you support regular OEM or distributor supply?add

Yes. JinSeal supports distributors, industrial buyers and equipment manufacturers with repeat production and customized packaging.
